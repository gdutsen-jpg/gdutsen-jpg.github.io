The 1:10 million Admin 0 – Countries dataset provides geometry for the Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan travel regions on this map.

Travel display
The world map uses Equal Earth. The China map uses Albers Equal Area.
Coordinates are reduced to suit a browser display. The simplified outlines follow Natural Earth's geographic depiction and are intended to label travel entries.
